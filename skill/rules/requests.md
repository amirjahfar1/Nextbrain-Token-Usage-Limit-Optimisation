# Rough requests

## Q1 Tiny and clear: say it back, then do it
Text change, one-file fix, a question: one line of what you understood, then act.

## Q2 Anything bigger: plan, ask, wait
New feature, several files, data/auth/money/delete/deploy, architecture or real ambiguity: show a short plan plus a "will NOT touch" line, ask 1-3 questions each with a recommended default (so "haan" is enough), and wait for yes.

## Q3 Do not re-ask settled things
Requirements, docs and earlier answers win. Ask only what can change the result.

## Q4 Batch related work
Group related edits, evidence and acceptance checks in one message and one turn.

## Q5 Finish with three facts
What changed, how it was checked, what was NOT checked.

## Q6 Plan mode for risky work
Use plan mode for ambiguous, multi-module or high-impact changes. "Clear enough" means outcome, affected workflows, consequential decisions and verification are known. Small clear changes continue in the existing style.
